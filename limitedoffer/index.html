Questions before you start

What do I need to provide?

A completed brief, your logo and brand materials if you have them, and the information or images you want on the site. We can help shape copy from your notes.

Which six pages can I include?

We agree the page list before work begins. Home, About, Services, Work, FAQ and Contact are one common mix, but yours can be different.

How much will hosting and the domain cost?

They are billed separately. We will explain suitable options and their ongoing costs before you choose and connect them.

What happens after the included month of Care?

You can continue for £49/month if you choose, with no automatic renewal. New pages, redesigns and extra features are quoted separately.

Can you work with my existing website?

Yes. We will assess whether this fixed package fits, or whether a separate redesign quote makes more sense.

Can I add a shop or booking system?

A shop or custom booking system is outside this £500 package. Tell us what you need and we can quote it separately.

What do SEO, AI-search preparation and Google setup include?

We set page titles and descriptions, organise content clearly, add suitable markup, and help connect Search Console and analytics. We can help with a Google Business Profile if your business is eligible. Search rankings and AI recommendations are never guaranteed; Google controls its own verification timetable.
