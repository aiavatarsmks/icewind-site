The challenge

One website, two very different intents

Lamar needed one website to serve two very different intents. Patients needed to understand the clinic, practitioners, treatments and consultation route. Practitioners needed a clear path to academy courses and training enquiries. Both journeys had to feel premium, personal and trustworthy while remaining unmistakably part of the same brand.

The content also had to make a large amount of practical information easy to scan: team profiles, treatment pricing, city dates, credentials, clinic imagery and direct enquiry routes.

The approach

Two clear routes inside one brand

We shaped the site around two clear user journeys: Clinic and Academy. A concise hero introduces both routes immediately, while the navigation and calls to action keep the next step visible throughout the page. Both routes were settled during web design, before any code was written.

The visual system combines deep black, warm cream, raspberry and gold. Editorial typography and generous spacing give the brand a premium Mayfair character, while real clinic photography and video keep the experience human and grounded.

What we delivered

Everything included in the published project

  • A responsive single-page website for desktop and mobile
  • Clear Clinic and Academy content architecture
  • Team profiles and easy-to-scan treatment pricing
  • Direct Instagram enquiry paths aligned with the client’s operating model
  • Photo and video-led storytelling for the clinic experience
  • Gallery, credentials and location content that strengthen trust
  • Analytics-ready interaction and section-view events
  • A lightweight, zero-dependency Node delivery package with video range support
Key experience decisions

The four decisions that shaped the site

Two audiences, one brand

Clinic and Academy are introduced as separate routes without creating two disconnected websites. The visitor can understand the distinction in the first screen and move directly to the relevant content.

Personal conversion path

The website does not force an unfamiliar booking flow. Calls to action lead to a direct Instagram conversation, matching the way Lamar handles consultations and academy enquiries.

Premium without becoming distant

The dark hero and gold details establish confidence, while warm interiors, practitioner photography and short-form video make the clinic feel personal and approachable.

Practical information made scannable

Treatment prices, practitioner roles, offers and city dates are presented in structured blocks so visitors can find the details they need without reading dense pages.
Technical delivery

How the site is built

The published experience is built with semantic HTML, custom CSS and lightweight JavaScript. The delivery package includes a zero-dependency Node static server, HTTP range support for smooth video playback on Safari and iOS, responsive layouts, lazy-loaded imagery, scroll-reveal behaviour, analytics-ready events and Cloudflare Web Analytics. Framework-free web development keeps the delivery package small and easy to host.

The outcome

Published, live and coherent

Lamar Academy now has a published digital home that brings its clinic, team, treatments, training route and Mayfair identity into one coherent experience. Patients can move from discovery to a personal consultation, while practitioners have a clearly separated route to academy enquiries.
