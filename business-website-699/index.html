Questions before you start

What do I need to provide?

Your completed brief, logo and brand materials if available, plus the information and images you want on the site. We can help shape copy from your notes.

Which 15 pages can I include?

We agree the page list before work begins. Home, About, Services, individual service pages, Work, Reviews, FAQ and Contact are one possible mix. The blog index and article template are included separately.

What does the blog include?

The blog index, reusable article template and editor are additional to the 15 core pages. You can add posts without changing code. Regular article writing is not included.

What does priority scheduling mean?

We assign your project the next available priority production slot when the brief and materials are ready. We confirm the start and delivery dates before payment; priority does not mean a next-day or seven-day delivery promise.

Which hosting and domain are included?

Hosting is included for 12 months from launch, and one available standard domain for its first year. We suggest suitable names and register the domain in your name. Premium domains, special extensions and unusual hosting requirements are quoted separately. Renewal costs are explained in advance.

What happens after the first year?

Hosting and domain renewals are separate after the included first year. We explain the renewal options and costs before you decide; there is no automatic renewal of Website Care.

Do I need to pay the full £699 upfront?

No. The £699 website package is paid in two stages: 50% (£349.50) when the project starts and 50% (£349.50) before delivery. VAT is added separately to each payment. The first year of eligible hosting and standard domain registration, plus the first month of Website Care, are included.

Can I add a shop or booking system?

Shops, complex booking systems, integrations, paid third-party services and ongoing SEO are quoted separately. Tell us what you need before we agree the scope.

What do SEO, AI-search and Google setup include?

We set page titles and descriptions, organise content clearly, add appropriate structured data, and help connect Search Console and analytics. We can help with a Google Business Profile if eligible. Search rankings and AI recommendations are not guaranteed.
