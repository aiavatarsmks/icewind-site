Questions before you start

What do I provide, and what do the 15 pages and blog include?

Send your brief, available brand materials and site content. We agree up to 15 core pages before work begins. A blog index, reusable article template and no-code post editor are additional; regular article writing is separate.

What does priority scheduling mean?

Your project gets the next available priority production slot once the brief and materials are ready. We confirm start and delivery dates before payment; it is not a next-day or seven-day promise.

Which hosting and domain are included, and what happens later?

Hosting is included for 12 months from launch and one available standard domain for its first year, registered in your name. Premium domains, special extensions and unusual hosting are quoted separately. We explain renewal options and costs before the first year ends.

How are payment and Website Care handled?

Pay 50% (£349.50) plus VAT at project start and 50% (£349.50) plus VAT before delivery. The first month of Care includes up to one hour of small updates, form checks and support; after that it is optional at £49/month, with no automatic renewal.

Can I add a shop, booking system or integrations?

Shops, complex booking systems, integrations, paid third-party services and ongoing SEO are quoted separately before scope is agreed.

What do SEO, AI-search and Google setup include?

We set titles and descriptions, organise content, add appropriate structured data, and help connect Search Console and analytics. Google Business Profile help is available where eligible. Rankings and AI recommendations are not guaranteed.
