Privacy Notice

This notice explains how ICEWIND DALE CONSULTING LTD uses personal data when you visit the ICE WIND website, contact us or send an enquiry. It describes what the site actually does as of the date at the bottom.

Personal data we collect

When you send an enquiry. Your name, your contact details (email address, phone number or messaging handle, whatever you give us), what you tell us about your project or business, an optional website link, the page the form was on, and our later correspondence. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information through the form.

When you browse. Our hosting and network providers (Railway and Cloudflare) and Google Fonts receive the technical information a browser sends with every request, such as IP address, browser and device details and the page requested, and keep short-lived logs to deliver and protect the site.

Website statistics that run without consent. Plausible Analytics counts visits and page views for the whole site and measures a handful of named actions on the £499 offer page, such as a button click or a sent enquiry. It receives the page address without its query string, the referring site or campaign label, whether the visit came from a Google advert, browser, operating system, device type and approximate location derived from your IP address. It does not store your IP address, sets no cookies and creates a hashed, daily-changing identifier that cannot be linked back to you. Cloudflare Web Analytics, added by our network provider, reports page load timings together with the page address and browser version, also without cookies. Neither receives anything you type.

Analytics that run only with your consent. If you press Accept analytics in the cookie banner, Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity load and set cookies. Google Analytics measures visits, sources and key actions. Clarity records page interactions (clicks, scrolling, mouse movement) and produces heatmaps and session replays; all form fields are masked, so typed content is never recorded. Until you accept, and after you reject, neither tool is loaded.

We do not use advertising pixels, remarketing or behavioural advertising profiling on this website.

Why we use personal data and our lawful bases

  • To review and respond to your enquiry, prepare an estimate or discuss next steps. Legitimate interests in responding to business enquiries and, where applicable, steps taken at your request before a contract.
  • To keep records of communications and protect our rights where necessary. Legitimate interests and, where applicable, legal obligations.
  • To operate, secure and protect the website and forms from misuse. Legitimate interests in running a secure website.
  • To understand how the website is used and improve it with aggregate, cookieless statistics (Plausible, Cloudflare Web Analytics). Under PECR we rely on the exception for statistical purposes, which requires clear information and a simple way to object; both are on the Cookie Policy page. To the extent personal data is involved, our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in measuring the website's performance.
  • To analyse visits in more detail with Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity. Your consent, given in the cookie banner and withdrawable at any time.

We do not use enquiry details for unrelated marketing without your separate consent.

Service providers and recipients

We use the following providers, each acting on our instructions unless stated otherwise:

  • Railway (hosting) and Cloudflare (DNS, content delivery, security, the enquiry-processing service and the page-speed beacon described above).
  • Google Fonts for typography.
  • Enquiry delivery. When you send a form, a service we run on Cloudflare Workers accepts the enquiry, holds it in a delivery queue only until it has been passed on, emails it to us through Resend (an email delivery provider), and records it as a lead in Zoho CRM (Zoho Corporation B.V., European data centre). The queue entry is deleted once delivery succeeds.
  • Google Workspace / Gmail to receive and manage correspondence.
  • Plausible Analytics (Plausible Insights OÜ, Estonia; data hosted in the EU) for cookieless statistics, under a data processing agreement; Plausible does not use the data for its own purposes.
  • Google Analytics 4 (Google) and Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft), only with your consent. Microsoft may also use Clarity data for its own purposes under the Microsoft Privacy Statement, including to improve its products.
  • Clutch, whose review widget is embedded on a few pages and loads from Clutch's servers.

We may disclose information where required by law or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

International transfers

Railway, Cloudflare, Google, Microsoft and Resend may process data outside the United Kingdom, including in the United States. Where such a transfer is subject to UK data protection law we rely on an appropriate safeguard, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum, or on an adequacy decision. Plausible and Zoho keep the relevant data within the EU. Contact us for details of a specific transfer.

How long we keep personal data

  • Enquiries. Up to 12 months after our last meaningful contact, then deleted or anonymised, unless a client relationship, a legal obligation or a legal claim requires longer. The Zoho CRM record follows the same period. If an enquiry becomes a project, records are kept for the period set in the agreement or required by law.
  • Enquiry delivery queue. Deleted automatically as soon as the email and CRM copies have been delivered; a failed delivery is retried and the entry removed on success.
  • Plausible. We hold aggregate statistics for as long as we use the service. Plausible's visit identifiers change daily and the raw IP address and browser string are never stored.
  • Google Analytics 4. Cookies last up to two years; event-level data is kept for the retention period set in our account, aggregated reports longer.
  • Microsoft Clarity. Session recordings are kept by Microsoft for up to 30 days; cookies last up to one year.
  • Server and security logs. Short-term, as needed to run and protect the site.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to access, correct, erase, restrict or provide a copy of your personal data, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interests. To object to our cookieless statistics, use the control on the Cookie Policy page; to withdraw consent for Google Analytics or Clarity, use Change your cookie choice. For anything else, email hello@icewind.uk.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. We would appreciate the opportunity to address your concern first.

Cookies and automated decision-making

Our use of cookies, local storage and cookieless scripts, including what runs before and after your choice in the banner, is set out in the Cookie Policy. We do not use automated decision-making or profiling to make decisions about enquiries.

Changes to this notice

We update this notice when our tools or legal obligations change. The latest version is always on this page.
