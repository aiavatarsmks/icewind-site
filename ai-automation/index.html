FAQ

Common questions

What business processes can be automated with AI?
Common ones include answering repetitive enquiries, processing and summarising documents, extracting information, qualifying leads and moving data between systems. We look for tasks that are repetitive and rule-friendly.
Can AI connect to our existing systems?
Yes, where those systems offer an API or integration point. We add an AI layer to what you already use rather than replacing it.
Will humans still be able to review the output?
Yes. We design automation so that people can review, approve or correct results wherever judgement matters.
Can you build an internal knowledge assistant?
Yes. We build assistants that answer questions from your own documents and knowledge base, with sources.
Do we need a large amount of data?
Not necessarily. Many useful integrations work with your existing documents and systems rather than large custom datasets.
How do you handle confidential business information?
We treat your information carefully and agree how it will be handled before any work starts, so you always know where your data goes.
