FAQ

Common questions

Can you add online booking?
Yes. We integrate with your existing booking system or set one up, so clients can book themselves in at any time, day or night.
Can clients pay a deposit?
Yes. We can take a deposit at booking, which cuts no-shows and protects your busiest slots.
Will it work well on phones?
Yes. We build mobile-first, because nearly all barbershop clients book from a phone, and we check it loads fast.
Can you show a gallery of our work?
Yes. We build a gallery so new clients can see your cuts and fades before they book, which helps them choose you.
Will we show up on Google Maps?
Yes. We set up your Google Business Profile and local SEO so you appear when people search for a barber nearby.
Can you help with the photos and text?
Yes. We guide you on the photos that convert and can shape the wording, so the site sells the shop properly.
