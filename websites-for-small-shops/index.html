FAQ

Common questions

Do we need a full online shop?
Not to start. We can launch with a product showcase and simple ordering over WhatsApp or click and collect, then add a full checkout later if you need it.
Can customers order over WhatsApp?
Yes. We can set up WhatsApp or Telegram ordering, so customers order the way they already message, without a heavy checkout.
Can you set up click and collect?
Yes. Customers can reserve or order online and collect in store, which suits local shops well.
Can we update products ourselves?
Yes. We build it so you can change prices, photos and products yourself, without needing us each time.
Will we show up in local searches?
Yes. We set up your Google Business Profile and local SEO so you appear when people search for your kind of shop nearby.
Can you add payments later?
Yes. We can add online payments, a full checkout or automation when you're ready, so the site grows with the shop.
