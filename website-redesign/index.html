FAQ

Common questions

Will a redesign hurt our search rankings?
No. We protect SEO on migration: we keep or carefully map URLs with 301 redirects, migrate titles, meta and structured data, and check everything after launch so rankings are preserved.
Can you keep our current content and brand?
Yes. We keep what works (your content, brand and any pages that already perform) and improve clarity, structure, speed and conversion around them.
How long does a website redesign take?
Most redesigns take a few weeks, depending on the number of pages, the content that needs restructuring and how much new material is required. We agree the timeline before work begins.
Can you redesign on our existing platform, or move us to a new one?
Both. We can redesign in place on your current platform, such as WordPress or Shopify, or migrate you to a faster, easier-to-maintain setup, whichever suits the project better.
Will the new site be faster?
Yes. We rebuild for performance and Core Web Vitals (optimised images, clean code and sensible hosting) so the site loads quickly on mobile and desktop.
Do you offer a free audit before we commit?
Yes. We start with a free express audit of your current website (performance, SEO, structure and user experience) so you can see what a redesign would improve before deciding anything.
