Cookie Policy

This policy explains the current use of cookies and similar technologies on the ICE WIND website.

Cookies we use

We keep the website as light as possible. Cookies and similar technologies fall into two groups:

  • Essential. A small amount of technical information is processed by our hosting, font and security providers so the website loads and stays secure. These are necessary for the site to work and do not require consent.
  • Analytics (optional). With your consent, we use Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use the site — for example how many people visit, where they arrive from, which pages they view and how they navigate — so we can improve it. Microsoft Clarity also produces aggregated heatmaps and session replays, with all form fields and other sensitive content masked.

Your choice and consent

Analytics cookies are not set until you accept them. On your first visit we show a banner with equal Accept analytics and Reject options. Nothing non-essential is loaded before you choose, and you can change your decision at any time.

Third-party services

We use GitHub Pages to host the site and Google Fonts to deliver website typography. We use FormSubmit only when you send a project enquiry through the form; it is not loaded during normal browsing. If you accept analytics, Google Analytics is loaded from Google and Microsoft Clarity from Microsoft.

These providers may process limited technical information necessary to deliver their services. Please read our Privacy Notice for more information about how personal data is processed.

How to manage cookies

You can change your analytics choice at any time using Change your cookie choice, or manage and delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect how some third-party services work.
