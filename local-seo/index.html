Straight answers

What we will not do

Local search attracts shortcuts, and plenty of businesses have paid for them before. Here is what we decline, and what each one would actually cost you.

No fake or incentivised reviewsWritten, bought or bribed reviews breach Google's policies. Removal, a suspended profile and the loss of every genuine review you earned all land on your business, not on the agency that wrote them.
No fake offices or invented addressesA virtual mailbox dressed up as a branch is a listing built on something Google can verify and reverse. When it goes, so does the ranking history attached to it.
No doorway pages for every London neighbourhoodForty near-identical pages swapping in a postcode add nothing a reader wants, compete with each other for the same terms, and are exactly the pattern Google's spam policies name.
No bulk citation blastsHundreds of listings on directories nobody visits create hundreds of records to correct later. We would rather place fewer, on sites your customers actually use, and keep them accurate.

FAQ

Common questions

What does local SEO actually involve?
Your Google Business Profile, the local searches worth targeting, consistent business details everywhere they appear, citations on directories that real people use, a sensible approach to reviews, and pages on your own site that make your location and services clear. Plus the technical work that keeps all of it visible.
Can you guarantee we appear in the map pack?
No. Google decides what appears in the map pack, and proximity to the person searching is a large part of it, which nobody can change. We work on the things that are within our control: profile quality, relevance, consistency and the strength of your site.
Do you write or buy Google reviews?
No. Fake and incentivised reviews breach Google's policies, and the risk sits with your profile, not with us. We help you ask real customers at the right moment, respond well, and make review requests part of how you already work.
We serve clients across London but work from one address. Can you still help?
Yes. Google supports service-area businesses, and we set your profile up that way where it genuinely applies. What we will not do is create addresses or offices you don't actually operate from.
How is this different from your main SEO service?
Local SEO is the part aimed at people searching near you, including Google Maps and the map pack. Our wider SEO service covers technical health, content and rankings across all searches. Most businesses need some of both, and the work overlaps.
How do you report on local SEO?
We track local rankings, what your Google Business Profile is doing, and the traffic and enquiries reaching your site, then explain what changed and why in plain language.
