FAQ

Common questions

How much does a website cost?
It depends on scope. A one-page site is very different from a multi-section platform with custom features. We give a clear, itemised estimate once we understand what you need — and never quote a number that ignores the actual project.
How long does development take?
A simple site can be ready quickly; larger platforms take longer. We agree a realistic timeline before work begins and show progress as we go.
Can you redesign an existing website?
Yes. We regularly rebuild and modernise existing sites, keeping the content and structure that work and improving the rest.
Can you work with our existing branding?
Yes. We can build around your current brand and guidelines, or refine them where it helps the result.
Do you provide support after launch?
Yes. We offer monitoring, updates and improvements once you are live, at a pace that suits you.
Do we need a technical brief?
No. A short description of your goal and audience is enough to start. We turn that into a clear scope together.
