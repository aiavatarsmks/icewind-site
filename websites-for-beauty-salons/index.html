FAQ

Common questions

Can clients choose their treatment and stylist?
Yes. We set up booking so clients pick the treatment and their preferred stylist, with the right duration and price applied automatically.
Can you add online booking and deposits?
Yes. We integrate with your booking system or set one up, and can take a deposit at booking to cut no-shows.
Can you sell gift vouchers?
Yes. We can add gift vouchers so clients buy them online, which brings in revenue around birthdays and holidays.
Will it work well on phones?
Yes. We build mobile-first, because most salon clients book from a phone, and we check it loads fast.
Can you show a gallery and our Instagram?
Yes. We build a gallery of your work and can pull in your Instagram feed, so new clients see your standard before they book.
Will we show up on Google Maps?
Yes. We set up your Google Business Profile and local SEO so you appear when people search for a salon nearby.
