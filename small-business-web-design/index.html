A different project

Why a small business site is a different project

A small business is not a smaller version of a large one. The budget is different, the page count is different, and there is nobody in-house whose job is the website. A bigger build with more sections, more stakeholders and more moving parts is a different piece of work — that is our general web design service.

A smaller budget, spent deliberatelyThe money goes into the pages that bring work in. We say plainly what is worth building now and what can wait.
Fewer pages, agreed up frontMost small businesses need a handful of pages, not a large structure. We agree the list before anything is designed.
One or two jobs, not a wish listThe site exists to get an enquiry, a call, a booking or an order. Everything else on it supports that.
No marketing team behind itThere is nobody in-house to brief, review and feed the site. We keep the work on our side and the decisions on yours.
You are the one updating itPrices, photos and opening hours change. You should be able to change them in a few minutes, on a phone, without calling a developer.
Scope you can actually finishA project sized to a small business gets launched. An oversized one stalls half-built and never earns anything back.

FAQ

Common questions

How many pages does a small business website need?
Usually a handful: what you do, your services and prices, who you are and how to get in touch. We agree the page list before design starts, so the budget goes into the pages people actually open.
Can we update the site ourselves?
Yes. We build it so you can change prices, photos and text yourself, without needing us each time.
Do we need online payments or a full shop?
Not to start. Many small businesses launch with enquiry forms, click-to-call, WhatsApp or click and collect, and add a checkout later if the business needs one.
Can customers book online?
Yes, where booking suits the business. We integrate with your booking system or set one up, so people can book outside your opening hours.
Will we show up in local searches?
Yes. We set up your Google Business Profile and local SEO so you appear when people nearby search for what you do.
We have nobody in-house to run a website. Is that a problem?
No. We scope the site so it works without a marketing team behind it: few pages, simple editing, and support from us when you want a change made.
