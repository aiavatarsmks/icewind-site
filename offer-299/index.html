Questions before you start

What do I need to provide, and which pages fit?

Send a completed brief, available logo and brand materials, and the information or images for the site. We agree up to 7 standard pages before work begins; Home, About, Services, Contact, FAQ and service pages are common choices.

How are payment, hosting and the domain handled?

Pay £149.50 plus VAT to start and £149.50 plus VAT before delivery. Hosting and domain registration are separate costs paid directly to your chosen provider; we connect the domain at launch and you arrange renewals with the provider.

What happens after launch?

The first month of Website Care includes up to one hour of small updates, form checks and support. After that, Care is optional at £49/month with no automatic renewal.

Can I add a blog, shop or booking system?

A blog, shop, custom booking system, complex integration or pages beyond the 7-page limit can be quoted separately.

What do SEO, AI-search and Google setup include?

We set titles and descriptions, organise content, add appropriate structured data, and help connect Search Console and analytics. Google Business Profile help is available where eligible. Rankings and AI recommendations are not guaranteed.
