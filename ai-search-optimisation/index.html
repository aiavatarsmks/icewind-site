FAQ

Common questions

What is AI search optimisation?
It's the work of getting your business surfaced and cited when people ask AI tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google's AI Overviews, rather than only ranking in the classic blue links.
Is this the same as SEO?
It's related but not the same. Good SEO helps, but AI systems favour clear facts, structured data and citable content. We do both, and they reinforce each other: see our SEO service in London, or local SEO for London businesses if your customers are nearby.
How do you get us into AI answers?
We make your facts unambiguous, add structured data and an llms.txt file, keep your details consistent across the web and publish content that directly answers common questions.
Can you measure it?
Yes. We check how AI tools describe and cite your business before we start and track how that changes as we improve your content and structure.
Do we need a new website?
Usually not. We work with your existing site, improving its structure, content and markup. If the site is holding you back, we can redesign it too.
Where can I read more about your approach?
We've written a guide on how to get recommended by AI search, on our blog, which walks through the thinking behind this service.
