Common questions

A few things
worth knowing.

Useful information before you enquire, whether you have a repair in mind or a bigger change to plan.

What information should I send?+

A short description of the problem and your postcode are a useful start. If you know the make of a fitting or where a leak is coming from, include that too.

Can I get a quote before work starts?+

Tell us about the job first. Some work can be discussed from a description; other problems need an inspection before the scope and price can be agreed.

Do you cover my area?+

This example shows a local service area in south-west London. On a real website, the coverage list would reflect the business’s actual working area.

Can I supply my own taps or bathroom fittings?+

Mention that when you enquire. Compatibility, access and the condition of existing pipework need to be checked before installation is agreed.

Do you carry out gas or boiler work?+

Gas and boiler services are not advertised in this example. A real business should only list work it is qualified and registered to carry out.

Can I book a visit online?+

The enquiry form is a request to discuss a job, not a confirmed booking. A real business would get back to you to agree a visit.

What happens if the job changes?+

Any additional work or parts should be explained and agreed before the work goes ahead.

Is this a real plumbing company?+

No. Alder & Flow is a fictional business created by ICE WIND to demonstrate a ten-page website. Forms, phone and WhatsApp buttons do not contact a plumber.
